// ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW

Why Ghost Chat Stands Alone

Decentralized Relay Mesh & Signed Failover

Messages and call signaling route across decentralized WebSocket relays with automatic multi-relay failover and Ed25519-signed relay discovery. No central user accounts, no cloud message databases, and no single point of failure.

Multi-Device QR Companion & Simultaneous Ringing

Link Windows, macOS, and Linux companion apps via a 256-bit optical QR handshake (ephemeral X25519 + ChaCha20-Poly1305). Syncs chats, contacts, reactions, and simultaneous voice/video call ringing with zero cloud storage.

v3 Forward Secrecy & Sealed Sender

Every message uses a fresh ephemeral X25519 keypair, HKDF-SHA256, and ChaCha20-Poly1305 AEAD, wrapped inside an anonymous Sealed Sender wire envelope so relays cannot see who is messaging whom.

Encrypted WebRTC Calls & Strict P2P Mode

End-to-end encrypted DTLS-SRTP voice and video calls with native iOS CallKit/PushKit lock-screen ringing, automatic ICE reconnection, optional NAT-traversal TURN fallback, and a Strict Direct P2P Only privacy toggle.

60-Digit Safety Numbers

Verify active contact cryptographic identity with 60-digit numerical fingerprints and optical verification QR codes to mathematically guarantee zero man-in-the-middle attacks.

Zero Metadata & 18 Auto-Detected Languages

No phone numbers, emails, passwords, or usernames required. Automatically adapts to your device language across 18 languages with instant cross-platform QR and invite links.

Duress PIN & Anti-Tamper Auto-Wipe

Configure a secret Duress PIN to silently shred local message vaults while appearing to unlock the app normally. An anti-tamper lockout shreds local encrypted vaults automatically after 10 failed PIN attempts.

Encrypted .ghostkey Backups

Export portable cryptographic backups protected by PBKDF2 (100,000 iterations) key derivation and ChaCha20-Poly1305 authenticated encryption. Store your offline keys with total confidence.

View-Once Media & Ephemeral Timers

Send view-once photos that shred upon closing, and configure automatic self-destruct timers that permanently wipe chat messages from both devices after reading.

In-App User Protection & Instant Blocking

Compliant with Apple App Store Guideline 1.2 and Google Play User-Generated Content standards. Instantly block abusive contacts with a single tap to silence inbound traffic, or submit abuse reports directly through the built-in report dialog.

1-Tap Complete Local Device Purge

Purge all local AES-256 encrypted chat vaults, keypairs, media files, and linked companion tokens permanently in 1 tap from Settings. Nothing remains recoverable.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 27, 2026 | Published by Juvian Studio

Ghost Chat ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to absolute user privacy and sovereign security. This Privacy Policy details our decentralized, zero-knowledge architecture and strict zero-data collection practices in compliance with Google Play Store, Apple App Store, Microsoft Store, and international privacy standards.

1. Zero Central Data Collection

Ghost Chat operates as a decentralized peer-to-peer (P2P) client. We do not collect, store, track, sell, or monetize any personal data, contact lists, phone numbers, email addresses, message contents, location data, or analytical telemetry. There are no user accounts, no registration forms, no tracking cookies, and no advertising or analytics SDKs in the application.

2. On-Device Local Encrypted Storage

All message histories, contact lists, cryptographic keypairs (X25519), and application preferences are stored strictly on your local device's isolated storage using local AES-256 encrypted Hive vaults and ChaCha20-Poly1305 encryption. Hardware-backed secure storage (iOS Keychain, Android EncryptedSharedPreferences, and ChaCha20-Poly1305 encrypted desktop vaults) is utilized to safeguard your private cryptographic keys. Your private keys never leave your physical device.

3. Stateless Network Relays & Push Notifications (Zero-Knowledge)

To deliver encrypted messages and calls reliably across restrictive mobile networks and locked screens without operating any central user database, Ghost Chat connects only to minimal, stateless transport helpers:

  • Decentralized Encrypted Message Relays: Messages are encrypted client-side with per-message ephemeral X25519 Forward Secrecy (v3:) and wrapped in an anonymous Sealed Sender envelope before crossing public decentralized WebSocket relays. Relay endpoints are discovered via an Ed25519-signed read-only manifest.
  • Optional WebRTC STUN / TURN NAT Traversal: Voice and video calls connect peer-to-peer using DTLS-SRTP encryption. When direct P2P is blocked by strict cellular NATs or firewalls, the app may request temporary TURN relay credentials from a stateless, rate-limited edge worker. Users can disable TURN completely at any time via Settings > Privacy > Strict Direct P2P Only.
  • Apple APNs & PushKit Wake Delivery (iOS): On iOS, locked-screen call wakes and message alerts use Apple Push Notification service (APNs / PushKit) forwarded via a stateless, log-free edge relay. Push payloads contain only a generic wake alert or an end-to-end encrypted call offer—never plaintext message content, sender names, or user accounts.

4. Device Permissions Explained

  • Camera: Used strictly for scanning contact invite QR codes, scanning desktop companion pairing QR codes, and user-initiated peer-to-peer video calls. Camera frames are never stored or transmitted to any server.
  • Microphone: Used strictly for encrypted peer-to-peer voice/video calls and voice message recordings initiated by the user.
  • Storage / Media: Used solely to allow selecting file and media attachments to send over encrypted P2P connections, and for saving or importing encrypted .ghostkey password-protected key vault backups.
  • Notifications: Used solely to deliver local, desktop (macOS Notification Center / Windows Toast), or wake alerts for incoming encrypted messages or call invitations. On Windows and macOS, closing the main window minimizes Ghost Chat to the System Tray / Dock so real-time messages and calls continue working until you explicitly Quit.

5. Multi-Device Companion Linking & Zero-Cloud Sync

Ghost Chat offers companion desktop applications for Windows, macOS, and Linux. Linking a desktop companion is performed via an ephemeral optical QR code handshake using X25519 key exchange and ChaCha20-Poly1305 authenticated encryption over the peer mesh. No messages, private keys, or account data are ever uploaded to cloud servers. Users maintain absolute control and can revoke any linked desktop companion at any time from mobile Settings > Linked Devices, which immediately issues an authenticated remote wipe payload to purge the companion's local vault.

6. Children's Privacy Protection (COPPA / GDPR-K)

Ghost Chat is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect, request, or solicit personal data from anyone under the age of 13. Because our application is decentralized and operates strictly via local on-device encryption, no personal data is stored on external servers.

7. Account & Complete Data Deletion

Because Ghost Chat maintains no central user accounts or cloud databases, user data deletion is fully under your control. To delete all application data, select Settings > Delete Identity & All Data in the app, or uninstall the app. This permanently purges all local keys, linked device authorizations, and encrypted local Hive chat vaults immediately. For complete step-by-step instructions, see our Data Deletion Policy.

8. EU Privacy & ePrivacy Cookie Exemption Notice

This official website (juvianstudio.github.io/ghostchat) is a static informational website. It uses ZERO cookies, ZERO tracking pixels, ZERO web analytics, and ZERO third-party trackers. Store Support & Reviewer FAQ

Guidance for Apple App Store, Mac App Store, Google Play Store & Microsoft Store Review Teams

Q: Are demo login credentials required for review?

No. Ghost Chat does not require usernames, passwords, phone numbers, or email registration. When the app launches for the first time, it automatically generates a local X25519 cryptographic keypair on device and presents the chat interface immediately. Reviewers can test all features without credentials.

Q: How do review teams test messaging between two devices?

Reviewers can test between two test devices or emulators:

  1. On Device A, tap "Add Contact" to display your personal QR code and public Ghost ID.
  2. On Device B, tap "Add Contact" and point the camera at Device A's QR code (or paste Device A's Ghost ID string).
  3. The peer-to-peer session opens immediately and encrypted messages, photos, and calls can be exchanged.

Q: How do review teams test Desktop Companion Linking (Windows, macOS, Linux)?

Launch the companion app on Windows, macOS, or Linux. The desktop client displays a 256-bit optical pairing QR code. On your phone, go to Settings > Linked Devices > Link a Device and scan the desktop screen. The companion pairs securely within seconds via ephemeral X25519 ECDH + ChaCha20-Poly1305. Closing the window hides Ghost Chat to the Windows System Tray or macOS Dock so notifications and calls stay active; right-click the Windows tray icon > Quit Ghost Chat (or press Cmd+Q on macOS) to exit completely.

Q: How do review teams verify Apple Guideline 1.2 (User-Generated Content)?

Ghost Chat incorporates two distinct in-app mechanisms:

  • Block User: Inside any chat, tap the top menu or contact header and tap "Block Contact". The contact is instantly silenced and blocked from further messaging or calling.
  • Report User: Inside any chat, tap the top menu and select "Report & Block User". This launches an abuse reporting modal allowing the user to submit an abuse report directly to the developer team at juvianstudio@gmail.com.

Q: How do review teams test Data Deletion compliance?

Go to Settings > Delete Identity & All Data. The application confirms the purge and wipes all local AES-256 encrypted Hive vaults and cryptographic keys from hardware storage, returning the app to its fresh installation state.

Q: How do encrypted voice and video calls operate?

Calls are established between devices using DTLS-SRTP encrypted WebRTC with end-to-end encrypted signaling (v3: Forward Secrecy) routed over decentralized relays, optional stateless TURN NAT traversal (which can be disabled via Strict Direct P2P Only), and native iOS CallKit/PushKit wake support. Camera and microphone permissions are requested only when a call is initiated or answered.
