// ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW

Why Ghost Chat Stands Alone

Serverless Peer-to-Peer Mesh Network

Messages travel directly across decentralized peer-to-peer relay nodes (Waku protocol). No central cloud servers, no intermediate databases, and no single point of failure ever touches your data.

Double Ratchet Signal E2EE

Every chat session, image, voice note, and P2P call is cryptographically sealed using Signal Double Ratchet protocol end-to-end encryption.

Zero Metadata & No Phone Numbers

No phone numbers, emails, or accounts required. Your identity is represented strictly by your local public key string.

Automated Self-Destruct Timers

Configure automatic ephemeral timers to shred local message databases permanently from both devices once read.

1-Tap Complete Local Device Purge

Purge all local SQLite chat databases, keypairs, contacts, and media files instantly with a single tap in app settings.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 8, 2026 | Published by Juvian Studio

Ghost Chat ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to total user privacy and security. This Privacy Policy details our serverless architecture and strict zero-data collection practices in compliance with Google Play Store, Apple App Store, and Microsoft Store guidelines.

1. Zero Central Data Collection

Ghost Chat operates entirely as a serverless peer-to-peer (P2P) client. We do not collect, store, track, transmit, or monetize any personal data, IP addresses, contact lists, phone numbers, location data, or analytical telemetry.

2. On-Device Local Encrypted Storage

All message histories, contact lists, cryptographic keypairs, and application preferences are stored strictly on your local device's isolated storage using local database encryption. Your private keys never leave your physical device.

3. Device Permissions Explained

  • Camera: Used strictly for scanning QR codes to add contacts and conducting peer-to-peer video calls.
  • Microphone: Used strictly for voice and video calls initiated by the user.
  • Storage / Media: Used solely to allow selecting file attachments to send over encrypted P2P connections.
  • Notifications: Used to alert you of incoming messages or call requests.

4. Children's Privacy Protection

Ghost Chat is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect, request, or solicit personal data from anyone under the age of 13. Because our application is serverless and operates strictly via local on-device encryption, no personal data is stored on external servers.

5. Account & Complete Data Deletion

Because Ghost Chat maintains no central user accounts or cloud databases, user data deletion is fully under your control. To delete all application data, select Settings > Clear All Data in the app, or uninstall the app. This permanently purges all local keys and chat databases immediately.

Data Deletion & Account Purge Policy

Google Play Store Data Deletion Policy Compliance

Google Play Store policies require developers to provide clear instructions on how users can request or execute complete deletion of their account and associated data.

Ghost Chat operates entirely as a serverless peer-to-peer (P2P) application. We do not maintain user accounts, central cloud databases, email lists, or remote telemetry servers. Therefore, there is no user data stored on central servers to delete or request deletion for.

2. How to Completely Purge Your Local Data

Because all message histories, cryptographic keypairs, contacts, and media files reside strictly on your personal device's local encrypted storage, you have full authority over your data:

  • Option A: In-App 1-Tap Data Purge
    Open Ghost Chat > Go to Settings > Tap Clear All Data. This instantly wipes all local SQLite chat databases, media, and cryptographic keypairs from your device.
  • Option B: Device App Uninstall
    Uninstalling Ghost Chat from your mobile device permanently purges all local app data and sandboxed database files.

Store Support & Reviewer FAQ

Information for Users & Google Play Store Review Teams

Q: How do store reviewers test contact addition?

Reviewers can open the app and tap "Add Contact" to either scan a QR code or paste a public key hex string to establish a P2P messaging session.

Q: How does user blocking work in Ghost Chat?

Users can tap on any contact profile or chat setting to block and delete a contact. Blocked contacts can no longer send messages or call requests.

Q: Where are messages stored?

All database records are stored strictly in local encrypted SQLite files on the user's phone. No central servers or cloud backups are used.

Q: Are there any in-app purchases or subscriptions?

No. Ghost Chat is 100% free with no in-app purchases, zero ads, and zero monetization trackers.
